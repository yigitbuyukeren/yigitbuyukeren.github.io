Aile Cüzdanı

A free shared budgeting app for Turkish families.

Add your salary once; income, expenses, instalments, subscriptions and savings goals on one screen, visible to the whole family. The name means "Family Wallet".

Features ↓Architecture and showcase ↗
  • Tried on Android · iOS planned
  • React Native
  • Expo
  • TypeScript
  • PocketBase

ℹStatus: In development. It is not on the App Store or Google Play; it has been installed and tried on an Android phone, and the iOS build is not verified. Some recently added features (receipt photos, offline queue, widget) have not been tried on a real device yet. The source code is private. The backend runs on a server in Turkey. The family and numbers in the screenshots are demo data.

02 · Features

What it does

The app is entirely free: no ads, premium tier or in-app purchases.

  • Shared family budget for up to 5 members by invite code; a child role with limited permissions
  • Recurring income (salary) is added automatically
  • Instalment tracking; progress is calculated from the calendar
  • Subscriptions and planned payments with upcoming-payment alerts
  • Cash, bank and credit-card accounts; statement and due-day reminders for cards
  • Debt tracking, expense splitting and comments on expenses
  • Optional receipt photos, visible only to people who can see the budget
  • Foreign-currency entries (manual rate) and savings goals in gold or foreign currency
  • Shared savings goals
  • Category limits and an overall monthly limit with 80% / 100% notifications
  • 3, 6 and 12-month reports; the monthly report can be shared as PDF
  • Offline read cache; new entries are sent when the connection returns
  • Android widget and app shortcuts
  • App lock, full data export (PDF), account deletion
