Veil

Messaging without a phone number or email.

An Android client based on SimpleX. Veil uses the official prebuilt SimpleX core, verified by hash and signature, and changes the app layer. A Tor-only relay connection policy is implemented in that layer.

01 · Status

Closed alpha is the target.

The release decision is currently NO-GO. This is development work, not an audited or finished messaging product.

Implemented in the app layer

A fail-closed start gate and enforced Tor-only network settings. If Tor is unavailable, the app must not fall back to a direct connection.

Earlier checks

Unit tests and earlier emulator runs are documented in the repository. The latest changes have not been verified on a device.

Still unverified

Real-device behavior, all network egress paths and independent security review. Tor and encryption do not guarantee anonymity.

02 · Direction

What the first milestone aims to prove

Messaging

  • No phone number, email or central cross-chat user identifier.
  • End-to-end encrypted one-to-one messages and small private groups.
  • Offline store-and-forward delivery and locally encrypted chat history.

Evidence and limits

These are milestone goals. The repository tracks what is implemented and what remains unverified. Do not rely on Veil where your safety depends on it.
