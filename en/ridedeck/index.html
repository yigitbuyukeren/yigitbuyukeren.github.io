RideDeck

Mount your phone on the handlebars and turn it into a CarPlay-style riding dashboard.

An open-source Android riding dashboard for motorcycles. Speed, map, music and phone on one screen; glove-sized controls and no typing while riding. No accounts, no keys, no servers.

Highlights

Voice navigation

OpenStreetMap map, Valhalla motorcycle routing, up to three stops, fuel stations along the route. The far warning says the distance and a short "now" cue comes at the turn.

Messages read aloud

Notification messages are read only while RideDeck is on screen; each chat app can be muted separately.

Optional SOS

Off by default. Countdown after a hard impact and no movement, then a single SMS with your location link. Thresholds are not field-tuned; not a safety guarantee.

Music and phone

Cover swipe, in-app contacts, dial pad and call screen.

Alerts

Rain and ice alerts; the screen dims when the phone overheats.

Privacy

Data stays on the phone; only queries to free public map services leave it.

How it works

Built for motorcycles

  • No vehicle choice: Valhalla motorcycle routing, fuel search, crash thresholds and the 200 km/h dial are fixed for a motorcycle.
  • Landscape handlebar layout; dock on the left, fixed speed card, two configurable card slots (map, music, apps).
  • First launch asks for location only; phone, music and SOS permissions are requested where each feature is switched on.
  • Crash detection: hard impact + stillness + the phone tipping over (so potholes and red lights stay quiet). Runs with the screen off during a started ride; the countdown comes to the front as a full-screen notification.

Navigation

  • MapLibre + OpenStreetMap (OpenFreeMap), Nominatim search, Valhalla routing, automatic re-route.
  • Avoid highways and tolls; saved places (Home, Work, favorites) and "Go home".
  • Up to three stops, alternative routes, downloadable map areas. Stops survive a fuel detour, a road-preference change and an app restart. Routing and address search need internet; if a re-route fails offline you are told once and guidance follows the old route.
  • Three-stage prompts: "in 800 metres, turn right", the full instruction, a short "now" at the turn; "continue for 2 km" on long stretches. Messages and weather alerts never cut a turn instruction. Route requests carry your heading, so they do not start with a U-turn.
  • Arrival at the end of the route, when you stop at the door, or when GPS is lost in a covered car park; the ETA follows your actual pace.
  • Spoken guidance uses Android's text-to-speech and follows the phone language: Turkish on a Turkish phone, English otherwise. There are no voice commands or speech recognition.
  • Speed-limit display and spoken warning; the search box, dial pad and settings lock while riding (the lock reads a motion state, not raw GPS noise).

Safety

  • Weather: temperature, rain and ice alerts within an hour.
  • Heat protection: the screen dims above 42°C and goes dark when stopped.
  • SOS (off by default): automatic crash detection or long-press button; after the countdown, one SMS with your location to up to 3 contacts, no calls. Runs while the app is on screen or a started ride is active.

Phone and media

  • In-app phone: recent calls, contacts and dial pad; the dial pad is locked while riding.
  • Music: progress bar, swipe the cover to skip, volume keys.
  • Speed/clock strip over other apps. During a call a turn instruction becomes a vibration and is read once the call ends.

Install and privacy

Three steps

  1. Download the APK from the latest release and install it (allow unknown sources).
  2. Grant the location permission on first launch; the other permissions are asked for where each feature is switched on.
  3. Grant notification access under Settings > Permissions and system for music and message reading.

Privacy

No accounts or servers; data stays on the phone. The only things that leave it are queries to public services: search text (Nominatim), route and speed limit (Valhalla), approximate location for weather (Open-Meteo), fuel queries (Nominatim / Overpass). SOS only runs if you enable it and asks for SMS permission.

Note: This app can distract you while riding. Mount the phone securely, change settings while parked and follow traffic rules. The software is provided without warranty.

Open to contributions

Issues and PRs are welcome: new translations, offline routing and search, tablet layouts.
