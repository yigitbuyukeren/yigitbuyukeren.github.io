Aile Cüzdanı

A free, shared household budget app for Turkish families. Add your salary once and see income, expenses, instalments, subscriptions and savings goals on one screen, together with your family. The mobile client, server rules, notification scheduler and tests all live in one project.

26k+lines of code
176unit tests
230+HTTP integration checks
38database migrations

Problem

In my competitor review, shared household budget apps were not built for Turkey (no instalments or TRY support), and Turkish apps were weak on family sharing.

Approach

Instalment progress is derived from the calendar, and recurring income is added automatically. Access rules are enforced on the server (PocketBase), not in the client.

Result

Income, expenses, instalments, subscriptions, savings goals, category limits and reports all work. It is not in the stores yet; it is in local development and testing.
